THE IDEA

How your voice reveals the picture

1. We listen to your pitch. The microphone captures your voice, and a pitch detector estimates the musical note you are singing. We compare it with the target note at that moment.

2. Each note earns a score. Singing close to the target pitch for more of the note earns more credit. Easy mode allows a wider pitch range and gives you room to learn.

3. The picture is transformed. A 2-D Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) turns the image into spatial-frequency components. Low frequencies describe broad shapes; high frequencies describe edges and fine details.

4. Notes restore detail. As the song progresses, each note controls a band of image frequencies. Better singing restores more of that band; missed notes leave some noise behind. An inverse FFT turns those components back into a picture. The reveal keeps the best progress you have made, so it will not get blurrier when you miss a later note.

For a fair test, use headphones. If the song comes from speakers near the microphone, the mic may hear the backing track instead of your voice.
